Privacy Policy for EPHEM

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 | Last Updated: October 12, 2026

Core Privacy Guarantee: EPHEM (Application ID: com.ephem.chat) is built on an ephemeral, zero-storage peer-to-peer paradigm. We do not store, log, read, inspect, or retain your messages or chat transcripts on any central server or database. All conversations live exclusively in temporary volatile device memory (RAM) and are permanently purged upon disconnection.

1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the EPHEM mobile application (package name com.ephem.chat) developed and operated by Wali Khan ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled when you access and use our application on mobile operating systems, including Android via the Google Play Store.

2. Information We Collect and Process

A. Account Registration Data

To authenticate users and authorize access to private room signaling, we collect:

  • Email Address: Used solely as your account identifier and for authentication credentials.
  • Password Credential: Securely hashed with industry-standard cryptographic salt/hash routines managed by our authentication infrastructure (Supabase Auth). We never have access to your plain-text password.

B. Real-Time Signaling Information (Transient)

To enable direct peer-to-peer WebRTC connections between your device and other room participants, temporary signaling payloads are exchanged:

  • Session Identifiers & Room IDs: Temporary identifiers to match members of the same chat room.
  • WebRTC SDP & ICE Candidates: Temporary network routing metadata (such as IP addresses and port candidates) exchanged through an encrypted signaling channel strictly to establish the direct peer-to-peer connection.

Once the peer-to-peer DataChannel is opened, signaling channels are closed, and signaling metadata is discarded.

C. Chat Messages and Communications (Zero Server Storage)

Unlike conventional chat applications:

  • No Server Database Storage: Chat messages are transmitted directly between devices via WebRTC DataChannels secured with DTLS encryption.
  • Volatile RAM Only: Messages exist solely within volatile device memory. When you navigate away, leave a room, or close the app, the conversation is wiped.
  • No Chat Logging: We do not operate any database table or server log that records chat transcripts, message history, timestamps, or sender/recipient message pairings.

3. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

We work with trusted infrastructure providers to facilitate basic authentication and network traversal:

  • Supabase Inc.: Used for user authentication and private channel signaling. For more details on Supabase's data practices, review their privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google STUN Servers: Used strictly for standard RFC 5389 NAT traversal to determine public IP candidates for direct P2P connections. No personal payload is transmitted through STUN.

4. Device Permissions

The EPHEM Android application requests the following network-related permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to communicate with authentication and establish peer-to-peer WebRTC connections.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to detect internet connectivity and monitor active network transitions (e.g., Wi-Fi to cellular).
  • android.permission.CHANGE_NETWORK_STATE: Required by the WebRTC networking stack to manage peer socket connections.

We do not request or access sensitive device permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Location, or SMS.

5. Advertising and Analytics

EPHEM does not display third-party advertisements and does not integrate third-party ad networks, tracking SDKs, or invasive analytics tools (such as Facebook SDK or Google AdMob).

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard cryptographic and architectural measures:

  • All communication with our authentication and signaling services is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS / WSS).
  • Peer-to-peer WebRTC connections use Datagram Transport Layer Security (DTLS) and Secure Real-time Transport Protocol (SRTP).
  • Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device using encrypted keystore mechanisms (Flutter Secure Storage).

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Since message content is strictly ephemeral and never stored on servers, there is no message history to retain or delete. Your authentication account (email) remains in the authentication database until you request deletion.

To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated profile records, you may contact us at support@ephem.chat or submit a deletion request via our support channel. All account data will be permanently erased within 7 business days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for or directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove the account.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological or legal updates. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the application after changes constitute acceptance of the updated terms.
