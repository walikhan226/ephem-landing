Zero Server Message Storage • True P2P

Conversations That Live in Memory.
Gone The Second You Leave.

EPHEM creates temporary, direct peer-to-peer data channels between devices using WebRTC. Your messages never touch an application server database, are stored solely in volatile device RAM, and vanish entirely upon disconnect.

Designed for Total Privacy & Ephemerality

Built from the ground up to guarantee that your conversations cannot be harvested, logged, or subpoenaed from a central server.

⚡

Direct P2P WebRTC

Messages travel directly device-to-device through encrypted WebRTC DataChannels. No middleman relay inspects or records your communication payload.

🛡️

Zero Server Persistence

Our backend only negotiates the initial connection handshake. Zero message content, attachments, or conversation logs are stored in any database.

🧠

RAM-Only Memory Model

Chat history exists exclusively in your device's volatile RAM. Closing the app, leaving the room, or losing connection permanently purges the chat.

⏱️

Lamport Logical Clock

Deterministic message ordering is achieved mathematically through Lamport timestamps, immune to device clock tampering or cross-continent skew.

👥

Private Ephemeral Rooms

Create secure rooms with configurable capacity (2–5 members). Once all participants leave, the room identifier is immediately garbage collected.

🚫

No Ads, No Trackers

No third-party ad networks, no analytics trackers, and no background telemetry scripts. Your network traffic is strictly yours.

How EPHEM Works

A multi-stage protocol that coordinates discovery without exposing private communications.

1

Signaling Handshake

Peers briefly exchange SDP offers and ICE network candidates through a private Realtime channel to locate each other.

2

Direct P2P Mesh

Once connected, peers switch to direct, reliable, ordered WebRTC DataChannels secured with DTLS encryption.

3

Instant Memory Wipe

When the room closes or a member disconnects, state is wiped cleanly from volatile RAM. No database traces remain.
